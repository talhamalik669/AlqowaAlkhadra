Who We Are

Heavy haulage built for the GCC's hardest routes.

Registered with the Dubai Department of Economy & Tourism and the Dubai Chamber of Commerce, Al Qowa Al Khadra Transport specialises in moving heavy and oversized cargo where standard freight can't go — desert corridors, border crossings and last-mile industrial sites.

Our fleet of heavy trucks and trailers, paired with hands-on dispatch and real-time tracking, means every shipment is planned, escorted and delivered with full visibility from pickup to proof of delivery.
